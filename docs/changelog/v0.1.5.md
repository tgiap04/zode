## Highlights

- Control your Zode from another device through the end-to-end encrypted relay.
- A new orange app icon, and the app version in the status bar, menus and welcome page.
- Keep the display awake with a manual switch.

## Highlights (vi)

- Điều khiển Zode từ thiết bị khác qua máy chủ trung chuyển, dữ liệu được mã hóa đầu cuối.
- Biểu tượng ứng dụng mới màu cam, phiên bản app hiện trên thanh trạng thái, menu và trang chào.
- Giữ màn hình luôn sáng bằng một công tắc.

## Changes

### New

- Remote control through the relay, with a status bar icon and an entry in the account menu.
- List and resume past agent sessions from a paired device.
- A notification when a device asks to pair.
- Database: remove a downloaded driver; driver downloads are signed.
- Container list search, and spinners on rows being acted on.
- A manual switch to keep the display awake.
- A count of waiting agents on each project's rail square.

### Improved

- The active tab is marked in the brand orange.
- One size, spacing and icon for each role in Zode's own UI.
- Agent sessions keep their names after their tabs close.

### Fixed

- Signed-in sessions keep their rotated tokens safely.
- Remote control sessions stay alive under load.
- Terminal mirroring keeps the scroll region, cursor and charsets.
- Deleted sessions leave the lists at once.
